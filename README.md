# FUTURE_DS_01 - Business Sales Performance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the Data Science & Analytics Internship at Future Interns.

The objective of this task was to analyze business sales data and identify revenue trends, top-performing products, high-value categories, and regional sales performance. The analysis was conducted to generate actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-selling products
- Evaluate category-wise sales trends
- Compare regional sales performance
- Track key business KPIs
- Generate insights and recommendations

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Sales

- Region-wise Revenue

## 📈 Analysis Performed
### Revenue Trend Analysis
- Examined sales trends over time.
- Identified periods of high and low sales performance.

### Product Performance Analysis
- Determined the best-selling products.
- Analyzed product contribution to total revenue.

### Category Analysis
- Compared sales across different product categories.
- Identified high-performing categories.

### Regional Analysis
- Evaluated sales performance across regions.
- Identified regions contributing the highest revenue.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Top-selling products contributed significantly to overall revenue.
- Certain product categories consistently outperformed others.
- Regional sales performance varied across different locations.
- Sales trends revealed opportunities for business growth and optimization.

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products.
- Increase inventory for top-selling categories.
- Improve strategies in low-performing regions.
- Use sales trends to plan future business activities.
